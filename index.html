// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS //

How many sessions are included?

The displayed plans include 12 or 16 sessions per month, with corresponding totals for longer memberships.

How do I complete my subscription?

Choose a plan and send a WhatsApp enquiry. The coach confirms availability, payment details and the subscription.

Do I need an InBody scan?

The original site requests an InBody report. Ask the coach when it is needed and send personal health details privately, not through this public webpage.

Is training online or in person?

The original listing does not specify this. Confirm format, location and schedule directly with the coach before paying.
